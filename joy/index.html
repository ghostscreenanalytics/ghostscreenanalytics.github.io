Joy Rankings

A box score tells you who played well. Joy tells you who was the most fun to watch. It starts with Game Score, then adds a point for every three, steal, block and dunk. Don't like the formula? Change it, and the top 100 rebuilds as you go.

How this works

Regular-season and playoff games from 1996-97 through Nov. 2, 2026. Dunks are made dunks from the NBA's play-by-play, which is why the list starts in 1996-97. Wilt's 100 and other older games aren't ranked because the stats don't exist for them. The sliders re-rank a pool of the top games under every on/off mix of the four bonuses, so a game outside that pool can't appear.
